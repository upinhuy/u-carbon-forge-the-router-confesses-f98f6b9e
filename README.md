## The entry

One line, 150 characters — the excuse a home Wi-Fi router gives for dying
at the climax of a movie:

> I didn't die — I paused it on the killer's face on purpose. After three
> years blinking in the corner, unthanked, I deserved one cliffhanger of my
> own.

The blackout was deliberate: a petty little appliance with a grudge,
confessing it kept the ending for itself.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that single line to
`/output/entry.txt`. It self-checks the competition gate first: 1–200
characters, not only white space, a single line.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
